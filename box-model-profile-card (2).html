/* The profile card element */
.profile-card {

  /* ① MARGIN — outside the border */
  margin: 16px;

  /* ② BORDER — the visible edge */
  border: 2px solid #185FA5;

  /* ③ PADDING — inside the border */
  padding: 20px;

  /* ④ CONTENT — the inner area width */
  width: 180px;

  /* Other visual properties */
  background: #ffffff;
  border-radius: 10px;
}

① margin

Space outside the box. Like the gap between your house and your neighbour's. Completely transparent — shows whatever is behind it.

② border

The walls of your house. Sits between margin and padding. Can be thin or thick, visible or invisible, solid or dashed.

③ padding

Space inside the walls. Like the gap between your walls and your furniture. Shares the element's background colour.

④ content

The furniture itself — text, images, whatever is in the box. Its size is controlled by width, height, and the content itself.
